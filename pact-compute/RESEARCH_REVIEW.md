# PACT-Compute research review

Review date 12 November 2026. The three mechanisms are contract-to-protocol, finite-state auditing of pricing behavior, and sustainable allocation. The review searched each mechanism by method, recent benchmark and failure mode, followed references to the original papers, and checked the methods against the project's current implementation and evidence. It covered recent work through October 2026 plus earlier foundations directly needed for a method. Primary papers and author releases were used for technical decisions. The review is scoped to implementable methods and comparisons for this project.

## Contract-to-protocol

| Source | Method and relevance | Adaptation and evaluation in PACT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Li et al., 2025 | Extract intermediate annotations, then convert and check formal output. Their error analysis identifies oversimplification and fabrication. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01294v1) | Preserve exact spans, separate suggestions from validation, and retain ambiguities for review. Measure unsupported proposals and rules that change test outcomes. |
| Cosler et al., CAV 2023 | Map temporal subformulas to source language and enable local human correction. [nl2spec](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04864) | Give each rule and review finding an evidence link. Keep editable policy versions and show the original clause beside the interpreted rule. |
| Bisharat et al., 2026 preprint | Execution-grounded comparison evaluates specifications against traces and shows why parsing success is insufficient. [TLA+-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23425v1) | Score the booking protocol against accepted, invalid and incomplete traces with independent expected verdicts. Use state and transition coverage. The project uses a bounded executable protocol for its domain. |
| AeroReq2LTL, 2026 preprint | Formalization needs domain terminology, grounding and treatment of ambiguous requirements in an industrial dataset. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21715) | Keep explicit units, approval, temporal limits, and abstention. Test an ambiguous deadline and an unsupported waiver instead of inventing a clause. |
| Stipula, 2022 | Legal calculi distinguish parties, contractual state and actions with guards. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02353) | Treat producer, consumer and operator actions as state changes with actor and evidence. Separate an observed protocol violation from interpretation or adjudication of a legal agreement. |

The existing compiler has typed rules, source spans, human approval and deterministic validation. Its 180-case template regression suite tests recognized grammar. To claim protocol verification, it also needs tests where valid syntax produces an unsafe execution trace. Compare a presence-checking baseline with a temporal monitor using frozen holdout fixtures. Report false acceptance, false rejection, incomplete evidence and exact verdict accuracy. Groq extraction is an optional proposal source. An independent human-labelled corpus and external provider results are needed for generalization claims.

## Pricing behavior and learned automata

| Source | Method and relevance | Adaptation and evaluation in PACT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deng, Schiffer and Bichler, 2024 | Repeated pricing outcomes vary with learning algorithm and market setup. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02437) | Train independent sellers in a repeated market using realized sales and margin. Freeze policies and report held-out episodes under several strategies. |
| Learning Collusion in Episodic, Inventory-Constrained Markets, 2024 preprint | Capacity and finite sales horizons influence learned strategies. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18871v1) | Use a finite inventory and bounded episode, including scarce demand and exogenous supply changes. Keep its DQN/PPO experiments outside the tabular replication scope. |
| Epivent and Lambin, 2024 | Price wars can follow upward and downward deviations. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.econlet.2024.111661) | Pair downward and upward interventions with a sham trajectory, keeping demand, costs and availability fixed. Record both responses rather than interpreting a single cut as evidence of intent. |
| Den Boer, Meylahn and Schinkel, Management Science | Re-examines the learning and equilibrium assumptions behind apparent coordination. [Paper](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.08557) | Report multiple seeds and strategy heterogeneity. Keep frozen synthetic-policy labels distinct from live pricing evidence. |
| Algorithmic Collusion Without Deterrence, 2026 preprint | Distinguishes transient price responses, directional evidence and whether a deviation is profitable. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21867v1) | Record realized margin changes as well as price response. A motif is review evidence for a limited hypothesis. The experiment does not compute a globally optimal deviation or establish deterrence. |
| Verwer and Hammerschmidt, FlexFringe | Learn compact probabilistic finite automata by merging compatible states in observed prefixes. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16331) | Build a bounded history representation and merge compatible states using next-action distributions. Train on separate episodes and report held-out action accuracy, log loss, coverage, and state/edge counts. |

Keep the legacy auditor as a fixed heuristic comparison. In the research suite compare high markup, a simple context-matched detector and the intervention-linked finite-state auditor. Score injected response strategies, competitive controls and scarcity controls. Report precision, recall, false positives and abstention. Synthetic positives describe a configured response mechanism. Independently observed live patterns require review and retain their context.

## Sustainable allocation

| Source | Method and relevance | Adaptation and evaluation in PACT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radovanović et al., Carbon-Aware Computing for Datacenters | Preserve inflexible work while shaping flexible capacity using forecasts and service constraints. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.11750) | Use a common arrival stream and forecast availability in paired scheduler arms. Keep urgent workloads and deadlines visible. Score modeled emissions using the actual grid series after scheduling. |
| Flex, 2025 preprint | Separate planning and runtime choices with finite capacity, uncertain durations and delay budgets. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18357) | Plan with a finite lookahead and dispatch only the current slot. Compare a median duration forecast with a calibrated upper bound on the same workload. |
| Hall et al., 2024 preprint | Models risk and probabilistic performance constraints in carbon-aware operation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21510) | Estimate runtime coverage with held-out actual outcomes and audit missed deadlines. Report the trade-off when a larger safety buffer admits fewer jobs. |
| Romano, Patterson and Candès, NeurIPS 2019 | Conformal calibration corrects prediction intervals with held-out residuals. [Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/5103c3584b063c431bd1268e9b5e76fb-Abstract.html) | Use a one-sided split-calibration adaptation with chronological groups. Measure empirical test coverage. Changes in hardware and workload over time limit any exchangeability assumption. |
| CarbonScaler, SIGMETRICS 2024 | Exploits workload elasticity and marginal carbon cost rather than delaying every workload. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08681) | Keep workload flexibility explicit. Treat GPU-count elasticity as a future extension requiring workload-specific scaling measurements. |
| On the Limitations of Carbon-Aware Temporal and Spatial Workload Shifting, 2023 | Carbon benefits depend on location, duration and deadlines. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06502) | Report total and per-completed-job carbon, on-time completion and wait together. Keep the hindsight oracle separate from a deployable forecast. |
| Weng et al., USENIX ATC 2023 | Actual GPU-sharing traces describe fragmentation and heterogeneous demand. [Paper and trace](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc23/presentation/weng) | Use the public source rows for observed arrivals and resource requests, and successful lifecycle intervals as runtime proxies. Publish split membership and source hashes. |

The earlier 30-seed replay had 208 deadline flags in the carbon-aware arm and 209 in the policy-only arm. All were runtime overruns. The new comparison must distinguish runtime prediction accuracy, admission feasibility, and completed service. Live local hardware supplies measured runtime and board-level samples. Public replay still uses modeled energy and a region-specific grid history. Partner data remains necessary to evaluate an actual commercial exchange.

## Publication evidence

Report dataset versions and hashes, chronological or episode-grouped train/calibration/test splits, seeds, model and environment versions, baseline definitions, thresholds, sample counts and uncertainty intervals. Publish adverse outcomes alongside gains. Export confusion matrices, per-seed scheduler metrics, training histories, FSM transitions and the original evidence IDs. Report live results and controlled replay separately. The implementation adapts specific methods from the papers in a GPU booking setting, with quantitative comparisons against in-project baselines.
